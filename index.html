Frequently asked questions

How often can I donate whole blood?

Typically every 90 days for men and 120 days for women, though local blood bank rules may vary.

Is my phone number shared publicly?

No. Contact details are only released to a verified requester once you accept a specific appeal.

Does this replace the hospital blood bank?

No. Donations are always screened and collected by licensed blood banks or hospitals. Lifeline Drop only speeds up the connection.

What about plasma and platelets?

Appeals can specify the component needed (whole blood, platelets, plasma). Compatibility rules differ for plasma and platelets, so follow the hospital's instructions.
